**Screenshot Editor**

A lightweight screen capture and annotation tool for Windows.

**Capture Your Screen**

*   Fullscreen capture (F11) — grabs your entire screen (including multi-monitor setups)
*   Region capture (F12) — drag to select exactly the area you want, with a dimmed preview to help you see your selection

**Annotate with 11 Tools**

*   Select — click to select, drag to move, resize with handles, double-click text to edit
*   Pen — draw freehand with any color and thickness
*   Line — draw straight lines
*   Rectangle — draw outlined rectangles
*   Ellipse — draw outlined ellipses
*   Arrow — draw arrows with arrowheads
*   Eraser — paint white over mistakes
*   Fill — fill a small area with color
*   Text — click to place text with your choice of font and size
*   Callout — drag to create a text bubble with an arrow pointing to anything
*   Highlighter — draw semi-transparent highlighter strokes over important areas

**Colors and Thickness**

*   Quick-pick from 5 preset colors (Black, Red, Green, Blue, Yellow) or choose any custom color
*   Adjustable pen width from 1 to 50 pixels
*   Change color/thickness of any selected shape after drawing it

**Edit and Refine**

*   Undo/Redo — unlimited undo and redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y)
*   Delete selected shapes with the Delete key
*   Crop to a region you select
*   Resize the entire image by pixels or percentage, with aspect ratio lock
*   Clear All to start fresh

**Zoom and Navigate**

*   Zoom with mouse wheel or Ctrl++ / Ctrl+-
*   Fit to Window to see everything at once
*   Pan by holding middle-click, right-click, or Space + drag

**Multi-Tab Workspace**

*   Work on multiple screenshots at once with tabs
*   Each tab has its own independent image, zoom level, and annotations

**Save and Share**

*   Copy (Ctrl+C) the annotated image to paste into emails, chats, or documents
*   Paste (Ctrl+V) a clipboard image to open it as a new tab
*   Save (Ctrl+S) as PNG, JPEG, or BMP
